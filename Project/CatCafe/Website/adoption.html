Adoption Process

Our simple 4-step process ensures every cat finds the right home

1

Meet & Greet

Visit our cafe and spend time with our cats to find your perfect match.

2

Application

Fill out our adoption application form with your information and preferences.

3

Home Check

We'll do a brief check to ensure your home is cat-ready and safe.

4

Welcome Home

Complete the adoption and take your new furry friend home!

Adoption Requirements

✓ Be at least 21 years of age
✓ Provide valid identification
✓ Complete our adoption application
✓ Pass a brief home environment check
✓ Pay the adoption fee ($75-150 depending on cat)
✓ Agree to keep the cat indoors or in a safe environment
✓ Commit to regular veterinary care
