## The Sky is Always Changing

Clouds are the most visible, dramatic, and beautiful expression of our weather.

*   They are a giant physics experiment happening in real-time.
*   Observing them connects you to global processes: evaporation, wind, temperature, and pressure.
*   It's free art, every day.

---

## Cloud Formation 101

*   **Step 1:** The sun heats the ground, warming the air.
*   **Step 2:** Warm air rises (convection).
*   **Step 3:** As it rises, it expands and cools.
*   **Step 4:** Cool air can't hold as much water vapor, so it condenses on tiny particles (dust, pollen) forming a droplet.
*   **Step 5:** Billions of droplets = a cloud.

## The Cloud Primer: The Ten Genera

Classified by altitude and shape.

**High-Level Clouds (Prefix: Cirro- / ~20,000 ft)**
*   **Cirrus:** Wispy, feathery. "Mares' tails."
*   **Cirrocumulus:** Small, patchy, rippled. "Mackerel sky."
*   **Cirrostratus:** Thin, whitish veil. Causes halos around the sun/moon.

*(Show stunning example photos for each)*

---

## The Cloud Primer (Continued)

**Mid-Level Clouds (Prefix: Alto- / 6,500-20,000 ft)**
*   **Altocumulus:** Larger patches/rolls than cirrocumulus.
*   **Altostratus:** Gray/blue-gray sheet. Sun appears as a dim spotlight.

**Low-Level Clouds (Surface-6,500 ft)**
*   **Stratus:** Uniform, gray, overcast layer. Drizzle.
*   **Stratocumulus:** Low, lumpy, gray layer with breaks.
*   **Cumulus:** The classic "fair-weather" puffy cloud.

---

## The Cloud Primer (Continued)

**Clouds of Vertical Development**
*   **Cumulonimbus:** The king. The thunderstorm cloud. Anvil-shaped top.
*   **Nimbostratus:** The rain/snow cloud. Thick, dark, featureless.

## Predicting Weather with Clouds

*   **"Mackerel scales and mares' tails make tall ships carry low sails."** (Cirrocumulus + Cirrus = rain in 24-48 hours)
*   **"Red sky at night, sailor's delight. Red sky in morning, sailors take warning."** (Dust/particles scattering light)
*   **Building cumulus** in the morning can mean afternoon thunderstorms.

## Atmospheric Optics: The Magic of Light

When sunlight interacts with ice crystals or water droplets, magic happens.

*   **Rainbow:** Sunlight refracting and reflecting inside raindrops. (Sun must be behind you, rain in front).
*   **Double Rainbow:** A second reflection inside the drop. Colors are reversed.

---

## More Sky Magic

*   **22° Halo:** A ring around the sun/moon. Caused by light refracting through high, icy cirrostratus clouds.
*   **Sun Dogs (Parhelia):** Bright spots on either side of the sun, often part of the halo.
*   **Iridescence:** Brilliant colors inside a cloud. Caused by diffraction of light through similar-sized water droplets.

## How to Observe and Photograph the Sky

*   **Observation:** Just look up! Use a polarized sunglasses lens to cut glare and see details in the sky.
*   **Photography:**
    *   Use a wide-angle lens for vast skyscapes.
    *   Use a tripod for sharpness, especially at dawn/dusk.
    *   Never look directly at the sun through a camera lens!
