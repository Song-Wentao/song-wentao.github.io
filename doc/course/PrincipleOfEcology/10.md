### 2. The Ecosystem Concept

*   **Definition:** The sum of all the organisms living in a given area (the community) AND the abiotic factors with which they interact.
*   **The Two Fundamental Processes:**
    1.  **Energy Flow:** is **linear**. Energy enters as sunlight, flows through trophic levels, and exits as heat.
    2.  **Nutrient Cycling:** is **cyclic**. Chemicals are recycled between biotic and abiotic components.

---

### 3. Trophic Levels

*   **Primary Producers (Autotrophs):** Convert solar energy to chemical energy (photosynthesis). Base of every ecosystem.
*   **Primary Consumers (Herbivores):** Eat producers.
*   **Secondary Consumers (Carnivores):** Eat herbivores.
*   **Tertiary Consumers:** Eat secondary consumers.
*   **Detritivores/Decomposers:** Break down dead organic material, returning nutrients to the soil.

---

### 4. Energy Flow: The 10% Rule

*   **Laws of Thermodynamics:** Energy cannot be created or destroyed, but is converted to less usable forms (heat).
*   **Ecological Efficiency:** Only about **10%** of the energy stored in one trophic level is converted to biomass in the next level.
    *   The other ~90% is used for metabolism, lost as heat, or not consumed.
*   **Consequence:** This explains why food chains are short (rarely more than 4-5 levels) and why top predators are rare.

---

### 5. Nutrient Cycling: Biogeochemical Cycles

Nutrients move between **pools** (reservoirs) via **fluxes** (processes).

#### The Carbon Cycle
*   **Key Processes:**
    *   **Photosynthesis:** CO₂ → Organic Carbon (in plants).
    *   **Cellular Respiration:** Organic Carbon → CO₂.
    *   **Combustion:** Burning fossil fuels releases stored CO₂.
*   **Human Impact:** Burning fossil fuels is a massive flux, increasing atmospheric CO₂ and driving climate change.

#### The Nitrogen Cycle
*   **The Limitation:** Plants can't use atmospheric N₂ gas.
*   **Key Processes:**
    *   **Nitrogen Fixation:** N₂ → NH₃ (by bacteria, lightning, industry).
    *   **Nitrification:** NH₃ → NO₂⁻ → NO₃⁻ (by soil bacteria). Plants can use nitrate.
    *   **Denitrification:** NO₃⁻ → N₂ (by bacteria in anaerobic conditions).
*   **Human Impact:** Industrial fertilizer production (Haber-Bosch process) has doubled global nitrogen fixation.
