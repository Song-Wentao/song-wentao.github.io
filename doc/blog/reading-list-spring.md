Three books this quarter, each of which sent me somewhere I didn't expect.

## The one I disagreed with the whole way through

I won't name it here, but it argued a strong causal claim from what read to me like a correlational study. Worth reading anyway — it's a good example of how far confident prose can carry a shaky data point, and it made me go back and reread the original paper it was drawing from.

## The one that changed how I take notes

A short, unglamorous book about writing systems. Nothing revolutionary in any single idea, but the cumulative effect of taking it seriously for a month has been the biggest change to how I work all year.

## The one I'm still thinking about

Fiction, and I'd rather not spoil it. It's the kind of book where the last page rearranges everything that came before it.
